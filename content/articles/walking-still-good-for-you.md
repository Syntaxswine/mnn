In a development that has left the fitness industry reeling, it has emerged that walking, the thing humans have done since there were humans, remains good for you.

We know. We're shaken too.

## The shocking details

Walking, it turns out, is exercise. Not the kind with a membership. The kind where you leave the house, go around the block, and come back. Your heart works a little harder. Your legs remember what they're for. Your mood, mysteriously, improves.

Nobody is selling this, which is why nobody is telling you about it.

## The 10,000-step myth

Somewhere along the way the number 10,000 attached itself to walking like a barnacle. People hear it, realize they're not going to do it, and so they do zero instead. Here is the truth, from every doctor who has ever been asked: **some is better than none, and more is better than some.** That's the whole formula. There's no magic number. There's just the next block.

## The method (you already know the method)

1. Shoes.
2. Door.
3. Go left or right. Doesn't matter.
4. Come back when you feel like it.
5. Tomorrow, do it again.

> The best walk is the one you actually take.

## When to check first

If you've got a heart condition, bad joints, dizziness, or anything that makes walking painful rather than pleasant, ask your doctor what kind of movement makes sense for you. They will almost certainly say "walking," but they might say "walking, and here's how," and that second part matters.
