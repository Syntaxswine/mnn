We have been told there is a trick. One trick. A trick so simple, so powerful, that doctors across the country are sitting in their offices right now begging you to use it.

We investigated. We can now reveal the trick.

## The trick

**Take the pills. The ones they gave you. Every day.**

That's it. That's the whole trick. We're as surprised as you are.

## Why this is harder than it sounds

The problem with a daily medication is that it works quietly. Your blood pressure pill does not send you a text saying "Great job, no stroke today." Your cholesterol pill does not throw a party. The days you take it feel exactly like the days you don't.

So people skip. Not on purpose. They skip because Tuesday was busy, and then Wednesday they can't remember if they took it, so they don't, to be safe, and now it's Friday.

## The $4 device that fixes this

A pill organizer. Seven little boxes with the days on them. Fill it on Sunday, and for the rest of the week the question "did I take it?" has a visible answer. It is the least glamorous object in your kitchen and it will do more for you than every supplement in the cabinet combined.

## The secret trick inside the trick

Here's the part people get wrong. Sometimes a medication makes you feel lousy. A cough, a dizzy spell, an upset stomach. And the natural thing is to stop taking it and not mention it, because you don't want to be a bother.

> Stopping is fine. *Stopping quietly* is the problem.

Doctors have more than one pill for almost everything. If this one bugs you, say so, and they will very likely swap it. What they can't do is fix a problem they don't know about. They're not psychic. They're just doctors.

So: pills, daily, same time, organizer on the counter, and a phone call if something feels off. Doctors hate this one trick so much they will thank you for it.
